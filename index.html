❓ How to Play

🎥 Camera: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-click-drag to pan.

🏗️ Select a building, then click a tile in the 3D world to place it. A green highlight means buildable, red means blocked.

🏠 Residential houses population. 🏪 Commercial & 🏭 Industrial create jobs/income. 🎡 Services boost happiness. ⚡💧 Utilities power your city.

🔓 Unlock new cities sequentially with Empire Funds. Each biome has unique bonuses.

🌪️ Random disasters can strike — build Fire Stations/Hospitals or buy Insurance to reduce damage.

✨ Once you've earned enough lifetime funds, Prestige to reset your empire for permanent bonuses.

🌐 Submit your score to the global leaderboard to compete with other players!

🧨 Bulldoze removes buildings for a 30% refund.
